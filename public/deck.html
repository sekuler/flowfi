03 · CROSS-CHAIN

Bridge: LI.FI for any token, Circle CCTP V2 for native USDC.

Any token tab: LI.FI compares routes so you pick the fastest or cheapest. Native USDC tab: FlowFi calls Circle's own CCTP V2 contracts directly. USDC is burned on the source chain, Circle attests, and native USDC is minted on Arc, with no wrapped token.

◆

Built to be safe to interrupt: if a transfer stops after the burn, it can be resumed without burning a second time. The expected wait for each source chain comes from Circle's own finality docs.

04 · CORE TRADING

Swap: same-chain on Arc, routed through LI.FI.

Swap opens on USDC to EURC and lists the tokens LI.FI supports on Arc as quick picks. FlowFi is built to be a router, not a market maker: it sends you to real, deep liquidity instead of holding its own reserves.

05 · ANALYTICS

Dashboard: net worth, allocation, and activity mix.

Balances are read straight from the chain and activity from Arc's official Etherscan-run explorer. The portfolio split shows where your money sits, and the activity mix shows what you actually do: receive, send, approve, swap, bridge.

◆

No invented charts: the net-worth chart and 7-day change are built from daily snapshots saved in your own browser. Until enough days exist, the dashboard says "Not enough history" instead of drawing a curve.
