03 · CROSS-CHAIN

Bridge: gasless USDC, native EURC & cirBTC, any token via LI.FI.

Native USDC uses Circle CCTP V2 with the Forwarding Service: you sign once on the source chain and Circle mints on Arc, so no Arc gas is needed. EURC and cirBTC arrive natively through CCTP for non-USDC, and LI.FI covers everything else.

◆

Safe to interrupt: a transfer that stops after the burn resumes without burning twice, checked against Circle's usedNonces onchain.

07 · ONE BALANCE

Gateway: one USDC balance across Arc, Base, Ethereum and Arbitrum.

Deposit USDC on any supported chain and spend it on any other in seconds. Transfers use an EIP-712 burn intent and Circle's Forwarding Service, so no gas is needed on the destination.

◆

Works with both wallets: browser wallets sign the intent themselves; the Circle Wallet signs it server-side, limited to Gateway burn intents only.
