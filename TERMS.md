## What FlowFi is

FlowFi is a **frontend and router**, not a financial institution, exchange, custodian, or counterparty to any trade.

- **Arc Mainnet features** (Bridge, Swap, Dashboard, the mainnet AI Copilot) move real funds. Every transaction is signed by **your own connected wallet** — FlowFi never holds your keys, never custodies your funds, and never takes the other side of a trade. Bridge and Swap route through third-party infrastructure (**LI.FI** and **Relay**) that FlowFi does not operate, own, or control. Their terms and conduct are their own, not FlowFi's.
- **Arc Testnet features** (Circle Wallet, Token Launch, Liquidity Pools, CCTP, Gateway) use test assets with **no monetary value**. They exist to demonstrate FlowFi's own contracts and product ideas — nothing there involves real money, and nothing there is available on mainnet.
